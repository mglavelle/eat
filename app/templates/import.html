Google does not let an app like this read your saved places or Timeline straight from your account, so you download your own copy and upload it here. Each of you does this while signed in as yourself. The files stay on this computer.

1. Saved places and reviews

  1. Open takeout.google.com and choose Deselect all.
  2. Tick Maps (your places) and Saved, then create the export.
  3. Download the .zip Google emails you and upload it below, unopened.

2. Places you've actually been (optional)

  1. On your phone, open Google Maps, tap your picture, then Your Timeline.
  2. In the Timeline settings choose Export Timeline data and save the file.
  3. Upload that Timeline.json below.

From the Timeline, only stops that match a restaurant in the list are kept, as a date on that restaurant.
